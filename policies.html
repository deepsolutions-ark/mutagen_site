Research use only

Mutagen sells research compounds for in-vitro laboratory research. Our products are not drugs, foods, dietary supplements or cosmetics, and they have not been approved for use in people or animals.

By placing an order, you confirm that:

  • You are 21 or older.
  • You are buying for laboratory research only, and you won't use the products, or supply them to anyone else to use, in or on people or animals.
  • You know how to handle research chemicals safely, and you'll store, use and dispose of them properly.
  • You'll follow every law and regulation that applies to you where you live and work.

We don't give advice on dosing, preparation or use in people or animals. We may cancel any order if we believe the products won't be used for research.

Terms of sale

Prices are listed in USD and may change without notice. The price in your order confirmation is the one that applies.

We email payment instructions with your confirmation and ship once your payment clears. If something you asked for is unavailable, we'll tell you before you pay and adjust your order.

We may refuse or cancel any order.

We don't sell or share your information. To see or delete what we hold about you, email us.
